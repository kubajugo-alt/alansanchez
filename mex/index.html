Line wins pay left to right, as multiples of your line bet (total bet ÷ 9). Mex is wild and stands in for everything except Bonus. Three or more Bonus symbols anywhere pay on the total bet and start 8, 12 or 20 free spins, where every win is doubled. The machine returns about 97% over the long run.

Play credits only. No real money goes in or comes out.
