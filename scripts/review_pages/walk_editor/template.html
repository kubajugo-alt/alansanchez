The cast's new walks

Both lanes play as the game plays a walk: the planted foot holds still, the paintings cut from one to the next, and each painting lasts as long as its foot travels.

Build the cycle yourself: drag the steps into order (or use the arrows), tap a step and then a frame in the library to swap it, nudge a step to settle its feet, or mark a step as a missing frame and describe what it should be. Your cycle saves as you go; Approve tells Claude to put exactly this cycle in the game.
