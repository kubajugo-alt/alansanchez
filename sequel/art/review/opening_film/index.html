Elena's last drive

The bible's cold open, told as a short film of animated paintings instead of the playable prologue. It runs before the game starts at the airport. Every line in it is already recorded; the paintings move in small, slow ways, as the rooms do. Mark each shot and leave notes; nothing is made until you've been through it.

How it gets made, once you approve

  1. The 8 new paintings, in the game's painted style, from the bar and the barranco paintings as references. You review them as stills first.
  2. Each shot animated from its painting with Higgsfield (image to video, a few seconds each, silent), keeping the painting as it is: slow camera moves, cloud, water, light, small gestures. You review the clips.
  3. Cut together with the recorded lines, the radio, the surf and Elena's music; captions in the game's own lettering.
  4. It plays when a new game starts, can be skipped with a click, and ends on the title card, then the airport.
