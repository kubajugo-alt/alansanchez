# Dialogue review: Friday

Overall: sense 4/5, naturalism 4/5. Friday is the strongest-written day so far. The fight is in Vasco's voice (one-word answers when hurt, then a long, controlled confession), and the Rafa bar is light, warm and well paced; Chano's Telde story and the dusk monologue land without strain. The recurring problems are continuity slips that hurt plot logic: the lab placed "in the museum's basement" when the plot needs it somewhere else, Carmen's "lock" getting no reaction from a man whose family papers say "lock", Carmen's discs-as-mirrors told twice, and Yeray's mumbling being so explicit that Alan's non-reaction ("Sounds rough") reads as a plot convenience, not as a man who is distracted. One line breaks the bracket rule.

## S.fight
Sense 4/5 · Naturalism 4/5. The shape is right and Vasco is fully in voice; the Lorenzo speech is the high point. Alan's "everything at last" is meant to come out as a list, but it leaves out the one fact that makes Vasco's "go and be safe" follow: that the danger is to Alan himself. The lab's location also breaks Sunday's plot.
- `vasco_f_van_2` (Vasco) **must**: "I signed the transfer sheets myself. They're in the conservation lab in the museum's basement. You could have asked me. [pause] You could have just asked me." → "I signed the transfer sheets myself. They're at the conservation lab, in Las Palmas. You could have asked me. [pause] You could have just asked me." If the lab were in the museum's basement, Alan would check it himself that afternoon while he is down there with Carmen. On Sunday Carmen has to phone "a number Alan didn't know" to reach it, and Yeray says "they go to Las Palmas, to the lab".
- `alan_f_told_all_2` (Alan) **should**: "The tide a step higher every morning. My family's mark on a bell in Santa Ana. A sealed letter. I faxed it to Inês." → "The tide a step higher every morning. My family's mark on a bell in Santa Ana. A letter they left here, in 1751. Inês says it knows my blood." The letter isn't sealed any more (he opened it on Wednesday), "I faxed it to Inês" ends the list on its weakest item, and without the danger to Alan, Vasco's "If you're right... go and be safe" has nothing to answer.
- `vasco_f_2` (Vasco) **could**: "I know what you were doing in his office. You were looking for something to prove that someone was doing something wrong." → "I know what you were doing in his office. You were looking for proof that somebody was stealing from my cave." "Someone was doing something wrong" is vague filler from a man the voice sheet makes precise, and "my cave" carries his hurt.

## S.seaWall

## S.carmenCave
Sense 3/5 · Naturalism 4/5. Carmen is short, flat and tells the truth sideways ("a friend of mine thought..."), as she should. The explanation of how the cave works says the same thing twice, the key word "lock" goes by without the obvious follow-up from Alan, and the marriage line contradicts her backstory.
- `carmen_f_discs` (Carmen) **should**: "A friend of mine thought they were arrows. [pause] She thought the discs were mirrors. Each one keeping its own triangle lit." → "A friend of mine thought they were arrows. [pause] Not symbols. Instructions. Each disc keeping its own triangle lit." Two lines earlier Carmen herself has said the discs throw the light back onto the triangles, so presenting "mirrors" as the friend's theory makes no sense.
- `alan_f_carmen_3` (Alan) **should**: "Because I'm the only one who's said 'black mist' to you out loud." → "Because I'm the only one who's said 'black mist' to you. [pause] And my family's letter says 'lock' too." Alan has "Lock" from the founders' letter (Wednesday) and "to study the lock" from Inês (Thursday). Hearing Carmen's dead friend use the same word is the scene's biggest reveal, and he has to react to it (rule 2).
- `carmen_f_dinner` (Carmen) **should**: "I saw. [pause] The face of a man hit by somebody he loves. It's a particular face. I wore it for a year, in my marriage." → "I saw. [pause] The face of a man hit by somebody he loves. It's a particular face. I put it on my husband once." The bible's husband was "very nice. It was very boring", so she didn't spend a year as the hurt one. As the one who did the hurting she is talking straight to Alan, and that sets up "the one that stops minding".
- `carmen_f_beam` (Carmen) **could**: "From the spring equinox to the autumn one, el sol comes straight down that hole at noon, through a hole in the floor, onto seven black discs in the room underneath." → "From the spring equinox to the autumn one, el sol comes down through the roof at noon, and on through the floor, onto seven black discs in the room underneath." The previous line has just set up both holes, and "that hole... through a hole" trips the listener.
- `carmen_f_san_juan_3` (Carmen) **could**: "Everybody on la isla has always known that morning matters. Nobody remembers why." → "Everybody on la isla has always known that day matters. Nobody remembers why." The next line says the danger is at noon, so "morning" points at the wrong time of day.

## S.yerayLeaves
Sense 3/5 · Naturalism 4/5. Alan has just failed to reach the camp, and Rafa tells him the boy is "on the dig, up at Artenara". The narrator admits Alan should have asked, but Alan's reply then comes after the narrator line and seems to answer it, and nothing on screen shows why Alan lets it go.
- `alan_yeray_3` (Alan) **should**: "Hasn't everyone." → "Hasn't everyone. [pause] I'm not asking. If I ask, I'll be talking about Vasco all night." Also play it before `narr_yeray_go_3` (a code order change), so it answers Rafa's "a lot on his mind" and the narrator's line lands last. The added half shows the choice not to ask as Alan's own avoidance, which fits "someone with nothing to lose". The reorder alone needs no re-recording.

## S.talkRafa
Sense 4/5 · Naturalism 5/5. Rafa is light, direct and warm ("Drawing's never fair. That's what it's for."; "By the door. Where I can see you come in."), and the priest/confessions joke is a sly echo of Vasco. The conversation topics can be picked in any order, so one question can refer to something Rafa hasn't said yet. Yeray's fragments are a bracket breach and a mix-up over which "doctor" he means.
- `yeray_bar_2` (Yeray) **must**: "...down to the— the vault. The old granary. Under the caves. And he locks it. Click. [hiccup] And only he's got the key. Only him." → "...down to the— the vault. The old granary. Under the caves. And he locks it. Click. [pause] And only he's got the key. Only him." "[hiccup]" breaks the rule that [pause] is the only bracket allowed in a line, and a voice engine may read it aloud.
- `alan_ask_rafa_island` (Alan) **should**: "Why not go now? Madrid's not far." → "Did you never want to leave? Madrid, Barcelona?" If the player picks this topic before the paintings, Rafa has never mentioned Madrid or art school, so "Why not go now?" makes no sense. The new line works in either order and still sets up "You can't get light like this in Madrid".
- `alan_rafa_paint` (Alan) **could**: "And where would I go? Between the dog in the hat and the fisherman?" → "And where would you put me? Between the dog in the hat and the fisherman?" It follows Rafa's "I think I like it here too much", so "where would I go" first sounds like a question about Alan's life rather than the wall.

## S.talkYerayBar
Sense 3/5 · Naturalism 4/5. "Forget it, hombre. Forget it." is Yeray's voice. But he says "conservation" and "the lab" (Vasco's words from that morning) straight to Alan, and Alan's flat "Sounds rough" makes him look slow rather than distracted. "He" also slides from Vasco to Baute inside one line.
- `yeray_bar_6` (Yeray) **should**: "...and I say sí, doctor, sí, the lab, and I can't look at him... I can't. If I open my mouth I lose everything. Everything. He's been like a father to me. A father, you understand?" → "...and I say sí, doctor, sí, the lab, and I can't look at him... I can't. If I open my mouth I lose everything. Everything. El jefe's been like a father to me. A father, you understand?" As written, "he's been like a father" attaches to the doctor he can't look at, who is Vasco, there one month. "El jefe" points at Baute without naming him, so Saturday's napkin reveal is kept.
- `alan_yeray_2` (Alan) **should**: "Sounds rough." → "Sounds rough. [pause] Everybody's got a doctor they can't look at tonight." Alan turns what he hears onto himself and his own Dr. Teles instead of hearing the facts. That makes his deafness come from character, not from the plot, and it is the voice sheet's "jokes instead of feeling".
- `yeray_bar_1` (Yeray) **could**: "...once a month since Christmas..." → "...once a month since December..." The first disc came out on 20 December, before Christmas. It's minor, because he is drunk.
