# Dialogue review: The prologue and Tuesday

Overall: sense 4/5, naturalism 4/5. The prologue is tight, frightening and in good voice, and Tuesday mostly works: the comedy (Nieves, Gail and Derek, Candelaria, Chano, Montesdeoca) is sharp, and the key beats (Vasco's moment in the corridor, Baute's grandmother, Carmen on the steps, the mist) have the right shape and subtext. Most of the problems are continuity slips, not voice: how long Vasco has been on the island, lines for one day that leak knowledge from another (Derek, the arrivals board, the reception door), and a few examine lines reused in places where they no longer describe what is on screen. There are a handful of small voice slips (Chano saying "cariño", a stray "the beach").

Half of Europe comes here to turn pink. I give myself a day." It plays on Tuesday at arrival, before the beach, and the user ruled there is no sunburn talk at Gando: he isn't pink yet.
