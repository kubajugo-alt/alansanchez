# Dialogue review: Saturday (Alan)

Overall: sense 3/5, naturalism 4/5. The emotional spine is strong: the waking, the window and the two rules, Rafa's "I know" and the drawing, Inês refusing to absolve him, Pino and Lolo's collar/hair are warm, specific and in voice, and Alan's examine lines get shorter and stranger as rule 7 asks. Most of the problems are continuity left behind by cuts: lines that point at things no longer said (the fax Inês never mentions, "in your time", the radio that is "off"), a count that disagrees with the list on screen, and Nieves saying there are no messages when Inês has been ringing since eight. The other recurring problem is repetition: the "Vasco's up there with Baute, I have to reach him" beat comes three times in the day, and Alan tells Pino about the cistern again after telling her on Thursday.

Coming straight after Vasco is mentioned and with "sunburn" behind him, "him" reads as Vasco, and the next line then seems to answer the wrong lie.
- `alan_s_go` (Alan) **could**: "I could go now, before he turns round. Money on the table, out of the door, someone else by lunchtime. [pause] I always have." → "I could go now, before he turns round. Shoes in my hand, out of the door, someone else by lunchtime. [pause] I always have." "Money on the table" reads as paying for sex, which is not the joke or the guilt the scene wants.

## S.callSatScene
Sense 3/5 · Naturalism 4/5. Inês is dry, fast and unforgiving as she should be ("a disappointed nun", "find a way into one you should"), but the cuts to her letter-reading (`ines_call_sat_8`, `_13` to `_15`) left three lines pointing at things no longer said, and one that clashes with the letter's own rule.
- `ines_call_sat_last` (Inês) **must**: "No. [pause] I wish I couldn't have either. Here it is." → "No. [pause] I wish I couldn't have either. The rest's on your hotel's fax. Here's the end." With `ines_call_sat_8` cut, nothing tells Alan there is a fax, yet he says "Inês's fax" to Chano and asks Nieves for it.
- `alan_call_sat_3` (Alan) **should**: "I can't reach him. He's on the mountain. The radio's off." → "I can't reach him. He's on the mountain. There's no phone up there." He doesn't know of a radio yet (Vasco's Canal 9 note comes later), and that afternoon he reaches the camp by radio.
- `ines_call_sat_16` (Inês) **should**: "...Here, with the door opening, it's the reason it'll want you first." → "...Here, with the door opening, it's the reason it'll come for you." Alan has just quoted "it strikes first at whoever understands the lock", so "you first" contradicts the rule, and the blood-above-all line it depended on (`_14`) is cut.
- `ines_call_sat_17` (Inês) **should**: "So if it's opened in your time—" → "So if somebody has to stand in that water—" "In your time" quotes the cut `ines_call_sat_14`; as it stands the line means nothing, where the rewrite makes the cost of closing the door land on Alan.

## S.faxArrives

Makes the beat a late, horrible connection instead of a first reading he has already had.
- `nieves_fax_in` (Nieves) **could**: "Four pages, señor, an hour ago. The machine nearly gave up." → "Two pages, señor, an hour ago. The machine nearly gave up." Matches the two pages on screen (or add pages to the fax instead).

- `pino_s_missal` (Doña Pino) **must**: "Mira. My list. Two crossed out. Two not." → "Mira. My list. Five crossed out. Two not." The missal on screen strikes five of the seven, Chano has just counted them, and on Friday she said "Five crossed out" (`pino_f_1`).
- `alan_s_tell_pino` (Alan) **should**: "In Lisbon, in a cistern, it had me up to the chest. It knew my blood, and it backed away. [pause] And there's a bell in Santa Ana with my family's mark on it, Doña Pino." → "You know about the cistern. [pause] What I didn't tell you: there's a bell in Santa Ana with my family's mark on it, Doña Pino." He told her the cistern almost word for word on Thursday (`alan_th_pino_cistern`).
- `pino_s_1b` (Doña Pino) **could**: "I sat down to say a rosary and I fell asleep, and I woke up drowning in my own bed." → "I lay down to say a rosary and I fell asleep, and I woke up drowning in my own bed." She sits down and wakes in bed; "lay down" also pays off Alan's "Don't let her lie down".
