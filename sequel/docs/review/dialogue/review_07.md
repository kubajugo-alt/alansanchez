# Dialogue review: Sunday

Overall: sense 4/5, naturalism 4/5. Sunday is the best-written day so far. The packing and the queue are spare and inward, Carmen's door, Elena's message and the footwell land hard, and the grille ("He didn't make it.") and "Not now" are earned. The recurring problems are continuity: three lines say the dig radio has been silent "since Friday" or that there has been "not one message", but Alan raised the camp on Saturday and Lolo heard Vasco on the channel that morning. The day also loses track of its own count of the dead and its clock (a five o'clock departure that arrives under stars). Two reveals don't get their obvious follow-up: how Alan knows the cost, and who has the words of the song.

## room carmen_flat
Sense 3/5 · Naturalism 4/5. The door on its chain ("No." / "I'm an idiot.") and the closing and opening of the door are strong. The confession speech has one factual slip: it gets the number of dead wrong.
- `alan_su_idiot_4` (Alan) **must**: "Two people have drowned in their beds, and Vasco is up on that mountain with Rayco Baute, and I can't reach him." → "Six people have drowned since December, and Vasco is up on that mountain with Rayco Baute, and I can't reach him." Chano's paper counted six on Saturday, Alan wrote his own name under Pino's list, and "two in their beds" matches nothing he knows.

## S.elenaBox

Without the letter, Carmen's "the last one who did didn't come back" has nothing to stand on.

Without this, the obvious question (how does he know the closer dies?) goes unanswered, and Carmen's reply refers to something nobody said.

## S.whereIsVasco
Sense 3/5 · Naturalism 4/5. The Nieves call is excellent ("Burn it." "With pleasure." "Is he all right?"), and so are the deduction choices and Carmen's put-downs. The radio timeline contradicts Saturday: Baute answered Alan on the camp's channel at one o'clock, and Lolo heard Vasco on it that morning. The deduction also repeats Alan's door speech almost word for word ("only Rayco has the key"), and it leaves out the fact that makes Baute's guagua story a lie.
- `carmen_su_museum` (Carmen) **must**: "The museum. The guard tried the dig radio twice. Nada. [pause] The camp's been silent since Friday morning." → "The museum. The guard tried the dig radio twice. Nada. [pause] The camp's been silent since yesterday afternoon." The camp was on the air on Saturday (Baute answered Alan from it at one; the fuse went into Yeray's pocket after that).
- `alan_su_think_2` (Alan) **must**: "The radio's been dead since Friday. Not one message in two days, from a man who leaves notes about the coffee." → "The radio's been dead since yesterday. Two days, and not one word to me, from a man who leaves notes about the coffee." The current line contradicts Saturday's radio call and `alan_su_lolo`, which plays just before it (Vasco's "Tell Alan... Under.").
- `alan_su_lolo` (Alan) **should**: "Lolo heard him. Saturday morning, on the radio: 'The discs aren't going to the lab. They're going down. Under.' And Baute told me he'd gone down on the guagua." → "Lolo heard him. Saturday morning, on the radio: 'The discs aren't going to the lab. They're going down. Under.' And Baute told me Vasco took the guagua after noon. The only guagua goes at seven." In "he'd gone", "he" could mean Baute, and the line drops the point the deduction rests on: the lie.
- `alan_su_think_3` (Alan) **should**: "And Yeray, in Rafa's sketchbook, while I was busy not listening: the discs go down to the lower vault at night. The old granary under the caves. And only Rayco has the key." → "And Yeray, in Rafa's sketchbook, while I was busy not listening: every night this week, down to the old granary. 'The Portuguese doctor doesn't know.'" It repeats `alan_su_baute` from a few minutes earlier almost word for word. The napkin's other detail is new and points at Vasco.
- `carmen_su_lisbon_no` (Carmen) **could**: "His bags are at your hotel, cariño. Nobody leaves without their trowels." → "Without his trowels? Nobody leaves without their trowels, cariño." Carmen can't know where Vasco's bags are, and nobody on the call said.

## S.drive
Sense 3/5 · Naturalism 5/5. The dialogue is fine; the clock isn't. Carmen says "Vamos" in the middle of the morning, and the narrator then jumps to five o'clock. A drive of an hour or two from Las Palmas, leaving at five, can't come out of the cloud "into stars" in June (sunset is about 21:20), and it would reach the camp in daylight, not after Yeray's dusk visit.
- `narr_lr_1` (Narrator) **should**: "Five o'clock. The whole city going down to the beaches, and the Land Rover going the other way: up, into the cloud." → "Eight o'clock. The whole city going down to the beaches, and the Land Rover going the other way: up, into the cloud." With the time changed, the stars, the dusk cutaway and the dark camp all fit. BIBLE "Sun 23" says five as well and should change with it. The hours between "Vamos" and leaving still need a reason somewhere, even if it's only a word from Carmen.

## S.onTheRock
Sense 4/5 · Naturalism 4/5. It carries a lot of rules (open, shut, where and not when, someone in the water, noon), and it gets through them with real beats in between: the watch, the desk and "Not now." Vasco's explanation runs close to a lecture, but that is his voice when excited. Two small problems. Nobody asks who has the song. And the Tasarte story makes Baute "the boy", though he was twenty-six with a degree.
- `vasco_rock_8` (Vasco) **should**: "Where, yes. Not when. The order's in a song, and I don't have the words. [pause] And the wall can only be read from above, and the discs can only be set from below." → "Where, yes. Not when. The order's in the old women's song, and I don't have the words. Rayco might. [pause] And the wall can only be read from above, and the discs can only be set from below." The obvious next question, "then who has the words?", goes unasked. One hint sets up Monday's telling without giving it away.
- `vasco_rock_tasarte_3` (Vasco) **should**: "The site manager was twenty-eight. Charming. He gave the boy a cigarette and told him not to take it personally. [pause] Ignacio Montesdeoca. Who doesn't remember." → "The site manager was twenty-eight. Charming. He gave Rayco a cigarette and told him not to take it personally. [pause] Ignacio Montesdeoca. Who doesn't remember." Baute was twenty-six, a graduate two years younger than the manager, not a boy. Using the name keeps the injustice exact.
- `vasco_rock_6` (Vasco) **could**: "The one Rayco wanted, I never wrote down. [pause] It's in here. Only in here." → "How to open it, I never wrote down. [pause] It's in here. Only in here." It comes straight after how it shuts, so "the one" is ambiguous when heard aloud.
