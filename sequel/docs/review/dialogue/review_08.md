# Dialogue review: Monday

Overall: sense 4/5, naturalism 4/5. Monday is the strongest-written day: the finale's mechanics are said aloud by the right people at the right time, Vasco's rim-calls are short and urgent, Carmen's wing mirror and Baute's collapse land, and the confession, the trowel, Nieves and Chano end it in the right register. The problems are small and mostly local: one continuity error with Game I in Alan's speech to Baute, a few lines whose referent is unclear at the moment they're heard ("He's sending the sun back", "So this is how she went", "Tell him. Today."), a time slip or two ("last week", "this morning"), one repeated beat (the Guardia "in an hour" twice), and a confession branch where Vasco says what doesn't hurt but never what does.

## S.startMon
Sense 4/5 · Naturalism 4/5. The dawn shape is right: Baute calm and certain, Vasco's "nearly right... the worst thing you could have done", Alan's answer with Vicente as his best moment of the week. Two lines get the facts wrong and one key line has an unclear subject.
- `alan_m_1d` (Alan) **must**: "When it came, it took him. It took half the people on the shore. It had me too, and it let me go, because of whose blood I've got. [pause] I don't think it'll do that twice." → "When it came, it took him. In his own cistern, in his own water. It had me too, and it let me go, because of whose blood I've got. [pause] I don't think it'll do that twice." In Game I the Sea took three people in locked rooms and Vicente in the cistern, and the tide turned with "nobody drowned"; nobody on a shore died, so this line breaks the first game's canon.
- `baute_m_1b` (Baute) **should**: "I watched Elena do it a hundred times. And I watched you do it, Vasco, last week, when you thought I wasn't looking." → "I watched Elena do it a hundred times. And I watched you do it, Vasco, on Saturday, when you thought I wasn't looking." Vasco moved the sun disc on Saturday, two days ago; nobody calls two days ago "last week".
- `vasco_m_twenty` (Vasco) **should**: "Look. Up the shaft, at a slant. [pause] He's sending the sun back. Without him the moon's no one's. And the sea's no one's." → "Look. Up the shaft, at a slant. [pause] Magec's sending the sun back. Without him the moons answer to no one. And neither does the sea." Straight after Baute's lines, "He" reads as Baute, and "the moon's no one's" is too cryptic for the one line that explains why the tipped disc opens the door.

## S.surge
Sense 5/5 · Naturalism 4/5. Carmen and Vasco are urgent and in voice; one narrator line gives the Sea a sulk.
- `narr_m_dog` (Narrator) **should**: "The water dropped back to his knees, sulkily, like a dog called off." → "The water dropped back to his knees, slowly, like a dog called off that hasn't taken its eyes off you." "Sulkily" gives the Sea a petulant, faintly comic mood, against the rule that it's never funny and never shown as a character with feelings; the rewrite keeps the dog and makes it a threat.

## S.bauteGives
Sense 4/5 · Naturalism 4/5. Carmen naming her street, La Isleta and the concrete in the sun is what breaks Baute, and "It was never going to listen. Was it." pays off Alan's dawn speech. One time slip, and one narrator line that a pause could carry.
- `carmen_m_street_3` (Carmen) **should**: "There'll be ten thousand people on it this morning, Rayco. Our people. Canarios. Asleep." → "There are ten thousand people on it right now, Rayco. Our people. Canarios. Sleeping off San Juan." The stain comes after noon, while the discs are going in, so "this morning" and the future tense are wrong; the rewrite also says why they're asleep on a beach at two in the afternoon.
- `narr_m_baute_silent` (Narrator) **could**: "Rayco said nothing." → cut; play a held pause on Baute. Rule 4: what a pause can show the narrator doesn't say, and this scene already has three narrator lines.

## S.closeLock
Sense 4/5 · Naturalism 4/5. The chain of hands, "No, you don't", the train and "One try" all land; the narrator lines are the ones rule 4 keeps. Two Alan lines have unclear referents, and one Vasco line stings in a way that may not be meant.
- `alan_m_so_this` (Alan) **should**: "So this is how she went. Alone in the water, when the door shut." → "So this is how she went. The one who stayed in the water, when the door shut." With Elena on everyone's mind all week, "she" reads as Elena, who died in her car on the road; the rewrite ties it to the sixth verse the player has just heard, and to the rule that closing costs the closer.
- `alan_m_today` (Alan) **should**: "Tell him. Today. Not when it's convenient." → "Nearly. [pause] Tell him, Alan. Today. Not when it's convenient." It comes straight after Yeray's "Is it over?", so it sounds like Alan answering Yeray or telling him to tell Baute; "Nearly" answers Yeray, and "Alan" makes the rest Alan saying Inês's words to himself.
- `vasco_m_twice` (Vasco) **could**: "I've let go of people before. I got on a train once, in the middle of the night, and let go of the best man I ever knew." → "I've let go of people before. I got on a train once, in the middle of the night, and let go of a good man without a word." Said over Alan just after pulling him out, "the best man I ever knew" ranks Lorenzo above Alan; if that sting isn't meant, soften it, and if it is, keep it.

## S.confessionTalk
Sense 4/5 · Naturalism 4/5. "Later is when things go wrong with us" is a strong, plain line, and the Montesdeoca answer is very Vasco. The shared "what Vasco knew" run has two weak lines.
- `vasco_m_knew_2` (Vasco) **should**: "Funny. Sad underneath. Like a man at his own party who doesn't know why he came." → "He said he was funny. Sad underneath. Like a man at his own party who doesn't know why he came." Without "He said", it isn't clear that Vasco is quoting Yeray's description; with no subject the line floats.
- `vasco_m_knew_5` (Vasco) **should**: "Then you came down the steps into the water to get me. And I thought: if he tells me, he'll tell me before I ask. And if he doesn't, he'll wait until I do." → "Then you came down the steps into the water to get me. And I thought: if he's the man I think he is, he'll tell me before I ask. If he isn't, he'll wait until I do." As written, the thought is circular ("if he tells me... if he doesn't"), and this is the line that sets up "Thank you for telling me first".
- `vasco_m_knew_3` (Vasco) **could**: "I lay in that vault all night and told myself that, over and over." → "I lay in that vault a night and a day and told myself that, over and over." He was in the vault from Saturday night to Sunday night, so "all night" undersells it.

## S.confess
Sense 4/5 · Naturalism 4/5. The three ways of telling it are distinct and true to Alan (plain, excuses, self-flagellation), and Vasco's "Don't make it so big that I have to comfort you... Stay in the room" is the best line in the scene. The excuses branch leaves its own question unanswered.
- `vasco_m_excuses_1` (Vasco) **should**: "[pause] I know I left. I know about the rum. [pause] That's not the bit that hurts." → "[pause] I know I left. I know about the rum. [pause] That's not the bit that hurts. You told him there was no one." The player (and Alan) will ask "then which bit?", and the bible's point, that the lie is the sin, is never said in this branch.
- `alan_m_plain_3` (Alan) **could**: "And then I lied to him about you. And I would have lied to you about him. On Saturday morning I was already working out how." → "I lied to him about you. And I would have lied to you about him. On Saturday morning I was already working out how." He lied before the sex, not after ("And then"), and he has already said it three lines earlier in confess_1c; dropping "And then" fixes the order and makes the repeat sound like insisting rather than forgetting.
- `alan_m_plain` (Alan) **could**: "I broke both of them. Both your rules. I didn't tell you first. And I did it on purpose." → "I broke both of them. Both our rules. I didn't tell you first. And I did it on purpose." Alan agreed to the rules in January; in the honest branch "your rules" sounds like him keeping them at arm's length.
- `alan_m_blame_2` (Alan) **could**: "I'm the worst thing that's ever happened to you. Worse than the Church. Worse than Rome." → "I'm the worst thing that's ever happened to you. Worse than the Church. Worse than that night train." Rome was Lorenzo, the good thing; the train is what Vasco regrets.

## S.ending
Sense 4/5 · Naturalism 5/5. Carmen and Rayco, the keys and the wing mirror, "Ask me again in Santo Domingo", Nieves's smile and "It is still terrible", and Chano's San Borondón are all exactly right. One beat is repeated straight after the radio.
- `carmen_m_tell_rayco` (Carmen) **should**: "They'll be here in an hour, Rayco. I told them you'd be waiting." → "It's done, Rayco. They're on their way. I said you'd be here." It repeats carmen_m_guardia ("They'll be here in an hour... I told them Rayco would be waiting") almost word for word, a minute later, in front of the same people.
- `chano_end_3` (Chano) **could**: "My cousin's husband's brother was out on his boat at sunrise this morning..." → "My cousin's wife's sister's husband was out on his boat at sunrise this morning..." This is the motif's last outing (rule 8); extending the exact phrase pays it off and still gives "he's a liar" its man.
