# Dialogue review: Across the days: puzzles, the phone box and Ines, background people, close-ups, refusals

Overall: sense 4/5, naturalism 4/5. The phone calls are the best writing in this part: Inês is fast, dry and never absolves him, the Pessoa openers land, and Alan's inventory and close-up lines follow rule 7 well (funny early, short and bruised by the weekend: the trowel, the drawing, "I'm putting the nails back"). The background people are funny and stay in one line each. The real problems sit on Saturday. Alan's radio call is set at one o'clock, but Vasco's radio message, which Lolo says he has already heard, cannot go out before the cave's noon at 14:04. Don Lolo, whose voice is "almost nothing" and whose first full sentence is meant to come at the end of the week, gets three long paragraphs here. Beyond that there are small continuity slips (Montesdeoca's age, where Yeray sat, the letter quoted two ways on the same day, the bell "cast in Lisbon and Las Palmas") and a few Inês lines whose logic doesn't hold.

## S.radioCall
Sense 3/5 · Naturalism 4/5. Baute is good on the radio: warm, sure of himself, needling ("You sounded tired on Thursday"). Catching him with the guagua's hour is a satisfying beat. But the clock breaks it. Saturday's cave work runs to the sun's noon at 14:04, and only after that does Vasco bridge the radio with the foil. So Lolo can't have heard "Tell Alan" before a one o'clock call. Baute's "He finished in the cave… after noon" also doesn't work at 13:00. Moving the call to four fixes all of it, and La Isleta at 19:04 still fits.
- `alan_s_radio_static` (Alan) **must**: "Canal 9. Static. [pause] On the hour, the note said. It's ten to one." → "Canal 9. Static. [pause] On the hour, the note said. It's ten to four." The timing contradiction above.
- `baute_r_1` (Baute) **could**: "Risco Caído. Baute. [pause] Alan! What a surprise. Over." → "Risco Caído. Baute. [pause] Mr. Sanches. What a surprise. Over." The exclamation is out of voice ("never raises his voice"). His usual "Mr. Sanches" is also colder, which is what we want from a man who has just drugged Vasco. (He can still slip to "Alan" in `baute_r_put` for the edge.)
- `alan_s_radio_lie` (Alan) **could**: "He's lying. Vasco's up there, and Baute's lying about where." → "He's lying. Vasco never came down. [pause] So where is he?" Says "lying" twice. This version asks the obvious next question (where is Vasco?) instead.

## The engine's refusals
Sense 4/5 · Naturalism 4/5. Funny and in voice. Vasco's set is especially good ("I'm not thinking about Alan."). ## say
Sense 4/5 · Naturalism 4/5. A strong comic chorus: each figure fits its spot, the flirts are light, Alan flirts back with the men, and the Spanish words come at about the right rate. A few slips.
- `alan_bg_beach_surfer_wax` (Alan) **should**: "I'd drown very happily. Is the wax included?" → "I'd be hopeless. Very willing, but hopeless. Is the wax included?" The beach is open all week. A drowning joke by the sea after Tuesday breaks rule 7 and "the Sea is never funny", in a game where people drown in their beds.

- `alan_bg_museum_cocktail_pair` (Alan) **could**: "That's very kind, but I'd tread on your feet. And possibly on his." → "Oh. That's… very kind. I'd tread on your feet. [pause] And possibly on his." With women Alan blushes. As written he's too smooth.
