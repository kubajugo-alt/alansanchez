# Cave 6: the lines to read (2026-10-30, second pass: less narrator)

The user, on the first version of this table: "over all, with your re-works there is way too much narrator. We don't need
that much description. Some things should simply be done by the characters, other just said by the characters - we
don't need to have it all described."

So the narration in the Cave 6 rework was gone through line by line: what the game shows (the room, the light columns,
the water, the clock, the women of light) or what a character can do (walk, kneel, a pose, a sound) is no longer
narrated; what the player needs to know is said by Vasco, Alan, Carmen, Yeray or Baute; the narrator keeps only the
time jumps, the swallowed numbers, Baute breaking, the stills at the end and the one glimpse of the Sea.

**The 199 lines of the rework (118 changed, 81 new in entry 106):**

|---|---|---|
| narrator lines played | 66 | 13 |
| narrator words | 2142 | 302 |
| character lines played | 133 | 157 (the same 133, and 24 new ones) |
| character words | 2672 | 3092 |

53 narrator lines of the rework are no longer played, and 5 older narrator lines in the same scenes; 19 lines of
the rework were shortened or rewritten to take over what the cut narration said; 24 new lines. The longest narrator line
left is 27 words. No character line was cut. Nothing here is recorded yet (the rework's lines play as captions);
ids are never renamed.

Also new, from the user's second note ("Vasco nudges. If you ask him 'Which one goes first?', he reads the matching scene
for the next verse without naming the sign"): once Baute has said the telling, Alan can ask Vasco at the rim "Which one
goes first?" (then "Which one goes next?"), and Vasco reads the scene round the next disc's triangle in the telling's
order, without naming its sign: 2 lines for Alan, 7 for Vasco (counted in the new lines above). Asked after the telling,
the question no longer reopens the telling page from Vasco (it stays in the notebook).
