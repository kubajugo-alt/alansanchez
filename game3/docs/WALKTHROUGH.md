# The Forgotten Rhythm: walkthrough (2026-11-04)

The shortest route through the game as it is built now. It follows the route the automatic test plays to the credits
(`S.SOLVE[0..9]` in `js/story/*.js`), so it should match the game step by step. Hotspot and item names are as the game
shows them. "Use X on Y" means picking X from the bag and clicking it on Y. Choices in quotes are the dialogue option
to pick; any option that isn't listed still works, but takes longer.

Stuck? The parrot in the hostal courtyard repeats something it overheard that points to the next step, and Mireya
calls the hint from the kitchen.

---

## Prologue: 1586 (playing Catalina)

**The river mouth**
1. Look at **the empty sky**, **the dead fish at the waterline**, **the dark water past the reef** and **the air**.
   The signs the Sea is coming.
2. Go **to the huts**.

**The huts**
3. Talk to **Antón**: he asks you to warn people (the tally starts).
4. Talk to **the Wolof boy** and pick "I believe you". He joins you.
5. Talk to **Biafara**: she has a wet cradle blanket for you to wash.
6. Go **to the river**. Use **the blanket** on **the river** to wash it.
7. Back **to the huts**. Give **the washed blanket** to **Biafara**.
8. Use **the cane trash**: the two drums are hidden in it.

**The square**
9. Give **the two drums** to **the mill brothers**.
10. Talk to **the fish seller**. Take **the canons' linen** and give it to her.
11. Talk to **Juana**. Everyone goes down to the shore.

**The shore in the storm**
12. Talk to **Antón** to play the rhythm. Keep the beat; if you miss, he says "again". It washes the sign away.
13. Take **a torch** to get wet ash. Use **the ash** on **the flat rock**: the rain takes it again.
14. Use **the ash** on **yourself** (Catalina). The prologue ends and you're in Lisbon.

## Lisbon (playing Alan, then Inês)

**The shop**
1. Look at **Os Maias, vol. 2**.
2. Go **up to the flat**. Take **the kitchen roll** and look at **Avô's photograph**.
3. Go **down to the shop**. Use **the kitchen paper** on **Os Maias**: three index cards (3 of 11).
4. Talk to **Inês**. Ask about "The safe", "Belgian" and "Pessoa I don't…" (6 cards).
5. Use **the safe, under the counter**: the deed.
6. Use **the post**. Give **the Património Sacro letter** to **Inês**.
7. Look at **the Post-it under the till**.

**The street**
8. Go out through **the street door**. Use **the electricity bill** on **the Multibanco**: the bill is paid.
9. Look at **the lintel over the shop door**.

**Vasco's office (as Inês, at the Patriarchate)**
10. Use **the FOUNDERS box file**. Talk to **Vasco**. Take **the crest paper**.

**The street again (as Alan)**
11. Use **the travel agent's** and pick "Three" tickets. You fly to Santo Domingo with the crest paper and the welcome
    fax.

## Wednesday: arrival

1. Check in at the hostal (it plays by itself).
2. **Cathedral nave**: look at **St Michael's offerings**.
3. Go through **the tower door**, then **up the steps** to the bell chamber.
4. Look at **the bell**, use **the bell**, then look at **the bell's sea side**, **the crack** and **the sea arch**.
   Night falls and you're in room nine.
5. **Room 9, at night**: take **your shoes**. Go to **the courtyard** and use **Room 4 (Vasco)** for the swap.
   If the parrot wakes up, you're sent back to room nine: try again.
6. Once the swap is done, use **the balcony doors**. Thursday begins, and you've found the 1586 coin.

## Thursday

**The cathedral archive (as Inês)**
1. Look at **the watermark album**. Use **the mislabelled boxes**, then use **Padre Ulises's torch**. You find the 1586
   note and the second line, and the game switches to Alan.

**Alan**
2. Look at **the ballplayers** (the frieze).
3. In the notebook, combine **the frieze sketch** with **where the mark looks**: you get the bearing idea.
4. **El Conde**: talk to **Yunior** and pick "San Antón", then "Married".
5. **San Antón**: talk to **Fello** and pick "Presidente" to play dominoes. Win.
6. Talk to **Fello** again and pick "cathedral bell", then "Thank you". You learn Tata is dead, and Tobías is in the
   shop.
7. Talk to **Tobías** and pick "Nod, once". This plays the river bench scene and gives you the worn phrase.
8. **Cathedral nave**: go **to the cloister**. The fight happens and you end up in Tata's alley.
9. **Tata's alley**: look at **the blue house's shutter** and talk to **Moncho**.
10. Go into **the blue house** and use **the photograph**. Friday begins.

## Friday

1. **The hostal kitchen**: use **the hurricane chart** (the day's pin). You learn Tobías dries the bell every night.
2. **Parque Colón**: look at **a bench** to read the watchman. Look at **his trousers**, **the puddle**, **the wet
   band** and **something by his boot**. This earns Batista's trust and shows the two bands.
3. **El Conde**: use **the sweet tray** to get the dulce de coco.
4. **The palace courtyard**: use **the stone bench**. Give **the dulce de coco** to **Moncho** and pick
   "grandfather": you get half of the lullaby.
5. **The hostal courtyard**: use **the fountain's tap**.
6. **The city wall**: Batista's dinner plays out. You get the pins marked and rule 5.
7. In the notebook, combine **the pins marked** with **the two bands**: you make the overlay.
8. Go **up to the roof** with Inês. Saturday begins.

## Saturday

1. Use **the hurricane chart**.
2. **San Antón**: talk to **Fello**, use **behind the counter**, then give him **the forty dollars**. You get the rum.
3. **The market hall**, then **the candle aisle**: talk to **Yolande** and pick "bell". At the drain, miss the first
   cue and then use the rum. You hear Yolande's story and do the vèvè. The game switches to Inês in the archive.
4. **The archive (as Inês)**: use **Chapter acts, 1930**, then **the 1930 folder**, then **the correspondence card
   index**. Look at **a card on the desk**, use **the card index** again, then **the outgoing copybooks**. You find
   the 1930 carbon.
5. **The palace dining room**: the dinner plays out (UNESCO, and how you defend yourself). Look at **your place
   card**, then take it. Go to **the courtyard**: you get Tata's notebook and the game switches to Alan on El Conde.
6. **El Conde (Alan)**: give **the linen jacket** to **Yunior**.
7. **The Blue Door bar**: talk to **Lalo**, then **the sisters**. They join you, and Sunday begins.

## Sunday

1. **The hostal kitchen**: talk to **Inês** (the breakfast and the napkin). Use **Tata's notebook** on **the kitchen
   table**: Mireya joins you. Mark the hurricane chart.
2. **The palace courtyard**: go into **the bishop's study**. Look at **a small photograph** and talk to **the
   bishop**. This plays the wall scene, and the napkin gets torn.
3. **The gallera**: talk to **Lilo**. Use **the rum** on **the red earth**, then give **Lilo** the rum and then
   **Tata's notebook**. You get the pages, the drum and the rhythm, and Lilo joins. The game switches to Inês.
4. **Inês**: use **the fax (to Paris)** to send the dossier. The game switches to Alan at sunset, and Tobías has gone
   up the tower.
5. In the notebook, make the prediction (the deduction that gives "prediction made").
6. **Cathedral nave**: go through **the tower door**, then **up the steps**, and save **Tobías**.
7. Use **the compass** on **the sea-side arch** to take the bearing. Night falls and Monday begins.

## Monday

1. **Parque Colón**: talk to **Tobías** (he gives you the message for Moncho).
2. **The candle aisle**: give **the rum** to **Yolande**. She'll come at two.
3. **San Antón**: use **Nando's door**. Talk to **Fello** and show him **Tata's notebook** (the margin).
4. **The Blue Door bar**: talk to **Lalo** and pick "Fello's coming". Lalo joins and gives you a note.
5. **The drying shed**: give **Lalo's note** to **Eddy**, then talk to him. You get the goatskin, and Eddy is charmed.
6. **The blue house**: look at **Tata's altar** and take **the little wooden boat**. Give **the boat** to **Moncho**,
   then use **the guava tree, through the window** to get the guava pegs.
7. In the notebook, combine **the margin** with **the wife's call**: the plan to find Carmela.
8. **San Antón**: talk to **Fello** and pick "Fello. When your wife…". Go to Tata's alley and back: the New York call
   comes and Carmela is found.
9. Use **Nando's door** again to read the message through the door. Give **the goatskin** to **Fello**: he makes the
   drum.
10. Go to **Tata's alley**. The raid happens and you're put in the police cell.
11. **The cell**: talk to **Fello**, then **Lalo**, then use **the water pipe**. Tuesday begins.

## Tuesday and the finale

**The hostal kitchen, at night (Alan)**
1. Use **the crest paper** on **the kitchen table**. Take **the glass pane in the door** and use it on **the St
   Michael candle**.
2. Use **the welcome fax** on **the kitchen table**, then **the wax seal** on **the candle**, then **the car keys** on
   **the table**.
3. Talk to **Vasco**: the letter is complete.
4. Talk to **Tobías** and pick "Tata said…". The game switches to Inês at the foundry.

**The foundry (Inês)**
5. Use **the crate** to find the hidden page, then talk to **Faustino**. The game switches to Alan in the square.

**The square (Alan)**
6. If the drums aren't back yet, use **the police pickup**. Use **the winch**: the bell goes back up.

**The map (Inês)**
7. Use **the 1890 harbour map**. Everyone goes down to the beach for the finale.

**The beach**
8. Use **the white chalk** on **the flat rock**, then give it to **Alan**.
9. **Alan**: talk to **Lilo**, then **Fello**, use **the Haitian drummers**, then talk to **Mireya** and **Nando**.
   The two halves of the lullaby meet.
10. Talk to **Moncho**. The finale moves to the shore steps.
11. **The shore steps**: talk to **Batista**, look at **the fortress wall**, then go **down to the beach**.

## Epilogue

1. Use **the palace gate**. You're back in the Lisbon shop.
2. Talk to **the Belgian at the counter**, then use **the telephone**. The credits roll.
